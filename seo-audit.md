- Location: HUDCO Colony, Kariyanna Building, Shivamogga.

- Location-based keywords: Custom jewellery designing in Shivamogga.

## 5. Keyword Usage Audit

The content cluster targets keywords related to:

- Custom jewellery designing in Shivamogga.
- Custom gold jewellery design.
- Custom silver jewellery designing.
- Custom jewellery designing process.
- Custom jewellery vs ready-made jewellery.

Keywords are used naturally in titles, headings and article content.

## 6. Internal Linking Audit

The pillar blog is intended to connect with all five supporting blogs.

Each supporting blog is intended to link back to the pillar blog.

Descriptive anchor text is recommended for all internal links.

## 7. Content Quality Audit

The generated content was reviewed against the following quality factors:

- Simple and understandable language.
- Relevant information for customers.
- Clear headings and subheadings.
- Useful FAQs.
- Local business relevance.
- Avoidance of unsupported business claims.
- Natural keyword placement.

## 8. Limitations

- Keyword search volumes and rankings have not been verified.
- Google search performance has not been measured.
- Actual search engine indexing has not been confirmed.
- Content performance requires monitoring after publication.
- Business information should be verified before publishing.

## 9. Future Improvements

- Publish the blogs on a business website.
- Connect the website to Google Search Console.
- Monitor impressions, clicks and keyword performance.
- Update content based on actual search data.
- Add relevant images with descriptive alt text.
- Improve content based on customer questions.

## 10. Conclusion

The SEO audit indicates that the project has a structured content cluster with keyword targeting, local SEO elements, metadata, FAQs and an internal linking plan.

The project provides a foundation for improving the online visibility of Vimala Jewellery Works.

Actual SEO performance will depend on website publication, indexing, competition and ongoing optimisation.
