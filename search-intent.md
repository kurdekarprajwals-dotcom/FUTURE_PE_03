# Search Intent Analysis

## Project
AI SEO Blog & Content Cluster Generator

## Business
Vimala Jewellery Works

## Target Location
Shivamogga, Karnataka

## 1. Informational Intent

Users want to learn about jewellery designs and the custom jewellery process.

Keywords:
- Gold jewellery design ideas
- How to order custom jewellery
- Things to consider before ordering custom jewellery
- Custom jewellery vs ready-made jewellery

Content Strategy:
Create informative blogs that answer customer questions.

## 2. Commercial Intent

Users are interested in exploring custom jewellery products and services.

Keywords:
- Custom gold jewellery
- Custom silver jewellery
- Personalized gold jewellery designs
- Custom silver jewellery designs

Content Strategy:
Create content explaining custom jewellery options and design considerations.

## 3. Local Search Intent

Users are looking for jewellery designing services in their area.

Keywords:
- Custom jewellery designing in Shivamogga
- Custom gold jewellery in Shivamogga
- Custom silver jewellery in Shivamogga
- Jewellery designing in Shivamogga

Content Strategy:
Include relevant location information and business details in the content.

## Conclusion

Understanding search intent helps create useful content that matches what potential customers are looking for.

The project uses informational, commercial and local search intent to plan SEO-friendly blog content.
