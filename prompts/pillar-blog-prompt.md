# Pillar Blog Generation Prompt

## Role
Act as an expert SEO blog writer and content strategist.

## Business Details
Business: Vimala Jewellery Works
Location: HUDCO Colony, Kariyanna Building, Shivamogga, Karnataka.
Products: Gold and Silver Jewellery.
Service: Custom Jewellery Designing.

## Main Blog Topic
Custom Gold and Silver Jewellery Designing in Shivamogga – A Complete Guide.

## Primary Keyword
Custom jewellery designing in Shivamogga.

## Objective
Create a detailed, informative and SEO-friendly pillar blog.

## Content Requirements

1. Write an engaging introduction.
2. Explain custom jewellery designing.
3. Discuss custom gold jewellery designs.
4. Discuss custom silver jewellery designs.
5. Explain the custom jewellery designing process.
6. Include important design considerations.
7. Compare custom and ready-made jewellery.
8. Include frequently asked questions.
9. Add a helpful conclusion.
10. Suggest internal links to five supporting blogs.

## SEO Requirements

- Use one H1 heading.
- Organize content using H2 and H3 headings.
- Include the primary keyword naturally.
- Use relevant secondary keywords.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.
- Write a suitable meta title.
- Write a meta description.
- Use simple and reader-friendly English.

## Important Instructions

- Do not invent business details.
- Do not make unsupported claims.
- Do not invent prices, offers or customer reviews.
- Focus on useful information for readers.
- Include Shivamogga naturally where relevant.

## Output Format

Provide:
1. Meta title
2. Meta description
3. Blog content
4. Frequently asked questions
5. Internal linking suggestions
