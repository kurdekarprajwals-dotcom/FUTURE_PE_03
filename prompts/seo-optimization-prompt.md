# SEO Optimization Prompt

## Role
Act as a professional SEO specialist and content editor.

## Business Details
Business: Vimala Jewellery Works
Location: Shivamogga, Karnataka.
Products: Gold and Silver Jewellery.
Service: Custom Jewellery Designing.

## Objective
Review and optimize the generated blog content for search engines and readers.

## SEO Optimization Requirements

1. Check whether the primary keyword is used naturally.
2. Identify relevant secondary keywords.
3. Improve the blog title.
4. Create an SEO-friendly meta title.
5. Create an engaging meta description.
6. Check H1, H2 and H3 heading structure.
7. Suggest relevant internal links.
8. Improve readability and paragraph structure.
9. Identify keyword stuffing.
10. Suggest relevant FAQs.
11. Include local SEO improvements for Shivamogga.
12. Recommend a suitable URL slug.

## Important Instructions

- Do not change the original meaning.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.
- Do not add unsupported business claims.
- Do not invent search-volume data.
- Keep the content natural and informative.

## Output Format

Provide:

1. SEO score with explanation.
2. Improved blog title.
3. Meta title.
4. Meta description.
5. Recommended URL slug.
6. Keyword placement suggestions.
7. Heading improvements.
8. Internal linking suggestions.
9. Readability improvements.
10. Final recommendations.
