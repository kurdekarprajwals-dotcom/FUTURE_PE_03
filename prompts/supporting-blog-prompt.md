# Supporting Blog Generation Prompt

## Role
Act as an SEO content writer and jewellery industry content strategist.

## Business Details
Business: Vimala Jewellery Works
Location: Shivamogga, Karnataka.
Products: Gold and Silver Jewellery.
Service: Custom Jewellery Designing.

## Objective
Generate informative supporting blogs related to the main pillar blog.

## Supporting Blog Topics

1. How to Choose the Right Custom Gold Jewellery Design.
2. Custom Silver Jewellery Designing – Designs and Ideas.
3. Things to Consider Before Ordering Custom Jewellery.
4. Understanding the Custom Jewellery Designing Process.
5. Custom Jewellery vs Ready-Made Jewellery – Which Is Better?

## Content Requirements

1. Write an attractive blog title.
2. Include a short introduction.
3. Explain the topic in detail.
4. Use H1, H2 and H3 headings.
5. Include relevant keywords naturally.
6. Provide useful information to readers.
7. Include frequently asked questions.
8. Add a conclusion.
9. Suggest an internal link to the main pillar blog.

## SEO Requirements

- Use simple and natural English.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.
- Include relevant local keywords where appropriate.
- Write a suitable meta title.
- Write a meta description.
- Maintain readability.

## Important Instructions

- Do not invent business information.
- Do not invent prices or offers.
- Do not create fake customer reviews.
- Avoid unsupported jewellery-related claims.
- Focus on useful and accurate information.

## Output Format

Provide:
1. Blog title
2. Target keyword
3. Meta title
4. Meta description
5. Complete blog content
6. FAQs
7. Internal linking suggestion
