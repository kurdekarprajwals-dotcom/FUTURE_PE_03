# Things to Consider Before Ordering Custom Jewellery

## Introduction

Jewellery is often chosen to express personal style and celebrate special occasions. While ready-made jewellery offers existing designs, custom jewellery allows customers to create pieces based on their own ideas and preferences.

Before ordering custom gold or silver jewellery, it is important to understand a few key factors.

## 1. Decide Your Jewellery Design

Start by deciding what type of jewellery you want.

You can explore:
- Traditional designs.
- Modern designs.
- Minimal jewellery.
- Personalized designs.
- Occasion-based jewellery.

You can also share reference photographs or sketches with the jewellery maker.

## 2. Choose the Material

Decide whether you prefer gold or silver jewellery.

Discuss the material specifications and purity with the jewellery maker before confirming your order.

## 3. Set Your Budget

Decide your approximate budget before placing an order.

Ask about:
- Material cost.
- Jewellery weight.
- Making charges.
- Other applicable costs.

Always confirm the final estimated price before approving the order.

## 4. Discuss Design Details

Explain your requirements clearly.

Discuss the size, shape, pattern and other design details to avoid misunderstandings.

## 5. Review the Final Design

Carefully review the proposed design before approving it.

Ask for changes if any part of the design does not match your expectations.

## 6. Confirm Delivery Time

Custom jewellery may require time for designing and creation.

Ask the jewellery maker about the expected completion date before confirming your order.

## 7. Keep Order Details Clear

Confirm the agreed design, material specifications, estimated cost and delivery timeline.

Having clear communication helps avoid confusion.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. What should I prepare before ordering custom jewellery?

Prepare your design ideas, preferred material and approximate budget.

### 2. Can I provide my own jewellery design?

You can share your own design ideas or reference images with the jewellery maker.

### 3. Should I confirm the price before ordering?

Yes. Discuss and confirm the estimated cost and applicable charges before approving the order.

### 4. How do I choose between gold and silver?

Consider your personal preference, intended use and budget.
